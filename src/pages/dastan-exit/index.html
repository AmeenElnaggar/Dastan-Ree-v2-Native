How an exit works

One deal, seen three ways

One assignment, two people it has to work for

An exit is not a resale. Nothing is marked up: the seller is made whole on the cash they paid, and the buyer inherits the contract exactly as it stands with the developer.

If you are selling

Recover your cash instead of losing a slice of it

  • 100% of what you paid, in cash, on completion of the transfer.
  • No overprice — which is exactly why a buyer moves quickly.
  • No cancellation deduction and no waiting years for a staged refund.
  • No public listing and no broker calls. Your file is shown only to matched buyers.
  • Zero commission. Dastan Exit charges the seller nothing.
If you are buying

Step into an older contract at its original price

  • The contract price, not today's launch price — the difference is yours.
  • Verified figures read off the contract and the payment receipts.
  • A plan already in motion: you continue the instalments as they stand.
  • An official transfer, approved by the developer and documented.
  • A 1.25% fee, charged only when the transfer actually completes.
