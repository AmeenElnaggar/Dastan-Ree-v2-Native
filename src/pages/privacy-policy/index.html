At Dastan Real Estate ("Dastan", "we", "us", or "our"), your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and services, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described below.

01 Introduction

This policy applies to information collected through our website, our property inquiry and unit-request forms, our contact channels, and any related communications. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we may link to but do not control.

We may update this policy from time to time, and the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically as you browse:

  • Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, preferred location and budget, and any message or details you share through our contact, inquiry, or unit-request forms.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, listings and projects you interact with, search filters used, referring pages, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Device & technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: small data files stored on your device, as described in the Cookies section below.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and connect you with relevant properties, projects, and developers.
  • Process and follow up on unit requests and viewing appointments.
  • Personalize the listings and content we show you.
  • Improve and maintain our website, security, and customer experience.
  • Send you updates, offers, and marketing communications where you have opted in (you may opt out at any time).
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

04 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as your selected language), measure site performance, and understand how visitors use our website. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function properly without them.

05 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it in the following limited circumstances:

  • With developers and partners: when you request information about a specific project or unit, we may share your inquiry with the relevant developer or sales partner so they can assist you.
  • With service providers: trusted vendors who help us operate our website, hosting, analytics, and communications, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Dastan, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

06 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

07 Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

09 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including developer pages, mapping services, and social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

10 Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the website after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, we'd be happy to help.
