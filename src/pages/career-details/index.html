Senior Property Consultant

Dubai, UAE Full-Time Sales Department

About the Role

Dastan Real Estate is seeking an ambitious, results-driven Senior Property Consultant to join our top-tier sales team in Dubai. In this role, you will be representing some of the most exclusive and luxurious properties in the region. You will be responsible for building lasting relationships with high-net-worth clients, advising them on market trends, and guiding them through complex real estate transactions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Act as an intermediary in negotiations between buyers and sellers, generally representing one or the other.
  • Advise clients on market conditions, prices, mortgages, legal requirements, and related matters.
  • Promote sales of properties through advertisements, open houses, and participation in multiple listing services.
  • Prepare documents such as representation contracts, purchase agreements, closing statements, deeds, and leases.
  • Develop and maintain a robust network of industry contacts to generate new business.

Requirements

  • Minimum 3 years of proven experience in luxury real estate sales in Dubai.
  • RERA certification is strictly required.
  • Strong negotiation skills, communication skills, and a high degree of emotional intelligence.
  • Fluency in English; knowledge of Arabic, Russian, or French is a significant advantage.
  • Valid UAE driving license and own vehicle.

Benefits

Uncapped Commission
Premium Health Insurance
Annual Flight Allowance
Latest Tech & Marketing Support
